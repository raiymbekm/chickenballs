# Chicken Balls Records

Updated 19 October 2026.

## Color palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Vivid violet | #6322F2 |
| Primary | Cool green | #34C978 |
| Extended | Golden yellow | #FFC42E |
| Extended | Midpoint red | #F94A40 |
| Extended | Blue | #2563EB |
| Neutral | Near-black | #101010 |
| Neutral | Near-white | #F7F7F2 |

Vivid violet and cool green lead the identity. Golden yellow provides a warm, saturated accent clearly distinct from the green. Midpoint red keeps some coral warmth; blue provides a crisp, cool accent. Use near-white text on violet and blue; near-black text on green, yellow and red. These solid-color pairings meet 4.5:1 contrast. Check contrast over animated backgrounds separately.

## Typography

- ChickenBallsFont.ttf: custom display headings, campaign titles and expressive artwork. Keep its proportions intact.
- Funnel Sans: body copy, track names, UI controls and the footer wordmark. Use regular 400, medium 500, semibold 600 and bold 700 where appropriate.
- Funnel Display ExtraBold 800: the current uppercase website hero title. It stays near-white in both themes.

## Logo

Use the supplied SVG silhouette. Preserve its proportions and leave at least one logo tile of clear space around standalone uses. Purple, green, black and white versions are allowed. Do not skew, crop or redraw the standalone mark.

## Shape rules

Use only sharp squares and rectangles, or rectangles with the same radius on every corner. Pills are allowed. Do not use leaf shapes or alternating corner radii.

- Large player and featured-release cards: shared 28 px radius.
- Featured release play button: 10 px on all four corners.
- Small controls: consistent rounded corners, or sharp rectangles.
- Album artwork: square.
- UI shape rules do not alter the silhouettes of service logos or illustrations.

## Icons and links

Use recognizable service marks for streaming platforms and social links. Preserve their silhouettes, provide accessible labels and tooltips, and change link color on hover. Show a service only when a verified destination exists for that track.

The previous custom pixel icons are preserved as editable SVGs in `assets/icons/pixel-backup/`. Playback, shuffle and theme controls can continue using the custom icon language. Platform marks are stored in `assets/icons/platforms/`.

## Visualizers

A uses two floating blooms with the original sound response, morphing between clover and organic forms mostly on the right. They drift and change size independently. Bass expands their centres; high notes create small outer ripples.

B uses travelling rows and expanding streaks with the original sound response. Keep tiles fully opaque and saturated with sharp edges against the background. Do not add feathering or gradients.

Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep hero text legible and idle motion calmer than music-driven motion.
