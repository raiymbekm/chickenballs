# Streaming link audit

Existing Spotify links were checked through Spotify oEmbed; Apple track IDs through Apple’s lookup API; 24 existing YouTube and 16 SoundCloud destinations through their oEmbed endpoints. New destinations were matched against the service’s artist/title listings. Availability may differ by region. Missing services are left out until an exact destination can be verified.
