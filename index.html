Our story

Eclectic electronic music brought to you by two lifelong high school friends from Astana.

2012

Epic Cheesy Toast

We started making music together while studying at the Astana Kazakh Turkish High School for Gifted Boys. Epic Cheesy Toast was our first shared experiment: two school friends finding their way into electronic music. The origin of the name is rather obvious for all KTL alumni (IYKYK) ;)

2014

Audionerds

We became Audionerds while living and studying separately in Turkey and Astana. The distance changed the way we worked, but the project kept us connected and the music kept moving forward.

2016

Fetch Quest

We renamed it Fetch Quest to signify that this endeavor would be the side mission in our lives, something positive and beneficial you do on your way — not your end goal. Despite this sentiment, our technical and musical abilities have gotten much stronger during this era and we produced tracks we're most proud of to this day. This is also when we took our marketing and design game to a whole new level.

NOW

Chicken Balls

All good things come to an end. Given the very dry musical spell in 2023–2026, the team has decided to end Fetch Quest. Despite Adilet producing his very best tracks to date during this time, keeping our musical legacy alive, and embarking on his solo venture — Peacepipe04, the duo agreed to bring Fetch Quest to a halt.

Initially, this decision was made to completely kill off the entire project. Yet, as time went on, we both realized that we wouldn't be able to give up making music — it's become an integral part of our beings. So, here we are again. Now much stronger but also less serious than ever, we've made the decision to revive our music release schedules and sick drop manufacturing (TBH only Raiko stopped due to having a kid, Peacepipe04 never really even took a pause).
